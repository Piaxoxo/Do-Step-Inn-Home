Located at Beingasse 13, Vienna, Be Free Hostel gives you a vibrant base in one of the city's most connected areas. From here, you can move easily between culture, shopping, restaurants, nightlife, and everyday local life while staying flexible and independent.

Perfect for city breaks, backpacking trips, and longer stays, this location lets you experience Vienna with energy, convenience, and freedom. It is a smart starting point for travelers who want to stay close to the action and explore the city their own way.

How you get in

A self-service house.
There is no reception.

Everything happens before you arrive, so nothing has to happen when you do. Land at midnight, walk straight in — nobody is waiting at a desk, and nobody needs to be.

1

Book online

Payment and registration are handled digitally. Nothing to print, nothing to sign at a desk.

2

Your check-in link arrives

By email if you booked with us directly. Booked through Booking.com or another platform? Then it arrives in that platform's message chat — check there, not your inbox.

3

Your QR code opens the door

Complete the check-in and the code lands on your phone. It opens the front door and your room, from 15:00 onwards, at any hour. No reception, no waiting, no curfew.

Trouble with the QR code or check-in?

Call us. Someone always picks up.

A door that will not open is the worst part of any trip, so we say this plainly: if your code fails or the check-in will not go through, ring this number and we will sort it out.

Easy, social, colorful.

Good to know

The honest bits

Better to read it here than to discover it at two in the morning. This is a real hostel in a real old building — here is what that means.

Quiet hours

It is an old building

Sound carries in the stairwell and the kitchen echoes. Quiet hours are respected, and if you are a light sleeper, take a capsule bed — it is the calmest option in the house.

Bathrooms

Showers and toilets are shared

On every floor, cleaned daily. No private bathrooms anywhere in the house — that is part of how the price stays where it is.

Kitchen

Cook whenever you want

The guest kitchen on the ground floor is open around the clock. Wash up after yourself and label what you leave in the fridge.

Times

Check-in from 15:00

Arrive any time after that, day or night. Ask us about leaving your bag if you land early or leave late.

Self-service

There is no front desk

Nobody sits in a lobby waiting for you. That is the point of the place — but the phone is always answered, so you are never on your own.

Groups

School trips and teams welcome

Write to us with your dates and headcount and we will put a quote together for the whole group.

Groups

Group bookings —
one quote, one invoice.

School trips, teams, choirs, clubs. Tell us your dates and how many you are, and we put together one quote for the whole group. Breakfast is not part of the room rate — we can book it for you around the corner.

Breakfast

Bookable at Felberstraße 20

Breakfast is not included in the price. Groups can add it at our sister house, Do Step Inn Home — a three-minute walk from the front door. Ask for it with your enquiry and it goes on the same quote.

One contact
