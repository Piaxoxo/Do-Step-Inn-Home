Chapter VI · From your door

Vienna starts on the doorstep.

Opposite Westbahnhof, one block off Mariahilfer Straße. Trams, trains and the airport bus are a two-minute walk. Here’s the city, measured from your bed.

  • Westbahnhof station 4 min walk · U3 · U6
  • Mariahilfer Straße shopping 1 block · ~6 min walk
  • Stephansdom & old town ~10 min by U3
  • Schönbrunn Palace ~8 min by U-Bahn
  • Belvedere Palace ~15 min by transit
  • Vienna Airport direct airport bus & CAT
